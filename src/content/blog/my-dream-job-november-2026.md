The more I "vibe code" apps and learn to create well build, high performance applications, the more I find myself gravitating to solutions for problems I see on local forums like Nextdoor and [Towne](https://towneapp.com).

Bus predictions, SMART Train predictions for both riders and drivers who get stuff in level cross traffic, and PG&E Outage reporting that focuses on the real user experience of checking information when you can't charge your phone. 

My goal has always been to help people with design and technology, not to generate revenue with growth tricks and advertising. Great user experience should come first and the rest should follow. 

## So, what would the dream job look like?

I recently applied for a Product Management job at the City of San Rafael, where I live. Having access to local government APIs to shift how it uses its data to better provide for its citizens sounds like a dream to me.

- Improved job boards and application tracking
- flood alerting,
- a better infrastructure reporting system than SeeClickFix that runs cheaper than contracting it to a company making $20m+ in profits,
- A city-based Marin transit, golden gate ferries, SMART train application for local commuters
- licensing our software to other cities to generate city revenue to better fund the city.
